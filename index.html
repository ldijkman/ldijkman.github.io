Tasmota Scan & Control

A free Android app that finds every Tasmota switch, plug and lamp on your Wi-Fi in seconds. Switch them, group them, make scenes, and give them timers with sunrise and sunset that run in the switch itself. Wall panel kiosk mode, and control from anywhere with Tailscale VPN.

No cloud, no account, no ads, no device limit.

Electra

An ESP8266 Wi-Fi relay with a TFT touch screen and mDNS, for timed lights and automatic garden watering. Hardware design on EasyEDA / OSHWLab.

Electra switches mains voltage. Handle it with care: it can kill you.
